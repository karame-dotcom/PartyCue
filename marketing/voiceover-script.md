## Option A: CapCut (free, easiest, all on your phone)

1. Install **CapCut**, tap **New project**, and import `partycue_demo_9x16_sfx_only.mp4`.
2. Move the playhead to **0.0s**, tap **Text → Add text**, and type the first line.
3. With the text selected, tap **Text-to-speech**, pick a voice, and tap **✓**. CapCut adds the voice as an audio clip starting there.
4. Delete the text box if you don't want it on screen (the video already has captions). The voice clip stays.
5. Repeat for each line at its start time. Drag a clip left or right if it overlaps the next one.
6. *(Optional)* Tap **Audio → Sounds** and add music at **10–20% volume**, or skip it and add a trending sound when you post on TikTok.
7. **Export** at 1080p, 30fps.

## Option B: ElevenLabs (most natural voice)

1. Go to **elevenlabs.io**, sign up, and open **Text to Speech**.
2. Pick a voice. Browse the **Voice Library** and filter for "young", "energetic" or "social media". Use the **Multilingual** model for the Taglish version.
3. Paste **one line at a time**, then generate and download each as MP3. Separate files are easier to place on the timeline.
4. For the announcer and judge lines, pick a second, more excited voice, or turn **Style** up and **Stability** down for more drama.
5. Import the MP3s into CapCut on top of `partycue_demo_9x16_sfx_only.mp4` and place each at its start time (steps 5–7 above).
